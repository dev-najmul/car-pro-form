Prototype Terms of Use

These terms explain how this reservation form prototype works. They apply to this demonstration and do not establish the transport operator’s live reservation, payment, or cancellation policies.

A demonstration reservation form

The form lets you explore a trip, select a vehicle and extras, enter contact details, and view a request summary. Submitting the form creates a local demonstration summary. It does not send a reservation, collect a payment, send an email, or confirm vehicle availability.

A request ID shown on the summary identifies the local demonstration only. It is not a confirmed booking reference. Contact the transport operator separately to arrange a real journey and obtain its applicable terms.

Prices and journey estimates

All prices are demonstration tariffs in US dollars (USD). They illustrate calculations using the selected vehicle, driving distance and duration, stop waiting time, service hours, extras, and gratuity. They are not a commercial offer or a confirmed fare.

Google provides the driving route estimates used by the Google-powered form. Stop waiting time is added by this application. Driving estimates do not include live traffic and may differ from actual travel conditions. The pickup date and clock time are separate trip details, not a traffic-aware arrival prediction.

The location service may be unavailable or unable to find a driving route. The normal form requires selected locations and a successful route calculation before it can show a calculated transportation price. The example mode uses clearly identified sample route metrics.

Google Maps features

Location suggestions, place details, and driving route calculations use Google Maps Platform services. Your use of these Google-powered features is also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Terms of Service. These terms are incorporated here for those features.

Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Read our Prototype Privacy Notice for the data this form stores and sends when you use location features.

Your draft

The application keeps a draft in your browser’s session storage for this tab. You can edit it before creating the demonstration summary. Starting a new request replaces the existing draft with an empty one; clearing this site’s stored browser data also removes the draft.

No production reservation database, payment processor, or email delivery service is connected to this prototype.
